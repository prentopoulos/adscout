# AdScout local workspace

Audience: one researcher on their PC. They review a ranked prospect, read its evidence,
approve or reject its draft, and inspect local CRM and message sandbox state.

The immediate MVP uses synthetic companies, scripted model answers and a fixture publisher.
All revenue is simulated and labelled. Live provider quality and remote integrations are
deferred. No real outreach or hosted deployment. PROJECT.md governs scope and gates.
