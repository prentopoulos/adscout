# AdScout recruiter walkthrough

Two-minute captioned portfolio video. Actual application captures use a separate synthetic
demo database. Company pages and model responses are fixtures; CRM and inbox are local
sandboxes; revenue is simulated. No live quality, time savings or sales lift is claimed.
No message is sent to a real recipient. The owner chooses where the finished files go.

## Six-slide deck

1. AdScout: AI-assisted advertiser prospecting for a publisher.
2. The business workflow: deciding whom to research, why they fit, and what to say.
3. The pipeline: enrichment, scoring, research team, writer/critic and human approval.
4. The review screen: actual draft, source quotes and approval controls.
5. The feedback loop: actual local CRM/inbox and explicitly simulated revenue.
6. Verification and limits: software evidence, scripted demo boundary and the next study.

Design follows the owner's supplied artifact examples: warm off-white, dark ink, purple
and blue accents, IBM Plex typography, large editable type, real application captures
and numbered process labels. No employer logo or claimed affiliation.
The video has captions rather than narration and is intended to work with sound off.

## Production and verification

Capture preview stills before assembly. Inspect every scene at the final aspect ratio;
ensure highlights refer to the visible control and captions do not cover source evidence.
Measure duration and inspect sampled final video frames before delivery. Keep the MP4 out
of Git until the owner selects its destination. Record final timings after encoding.

Completed: six editable Figma slides and a six-page PDF; a 120.0-second H.264 MP4 at
1600 × 900 and 30 frames per second, with captions and no narration. The final video
uses the timings above, verified against the encoded scene boundaries. Every scene was
sampled from the final MP4 and compared with its intended composition; full decoding passed.
Actual browser actions were performed on a separate synthetic demo database. No real
recipient, live model output, measured revenue or employer affiliation is represented.
IBM Plex typography follows the supplied references; its open font licence is retained
with the private build. Final artifacts remain outside Git pending the owner's review.
