# Workspace design

Mode: Operate. A researcher works at a desktop during an ordinary workday, comparing
company evidence and drafts. Use a light reading surface, dark teal navigation, restrained
teal actions, readable system typography and tabular numerals. Status text accompanies color.

The ranked table is the main navigation into a side-by-side draft and citations review.
Funnel and local inbox follow in the same page. Forms are explicit; errors explain recovery.
On small screens navigation becomes a row and review columns stack. Tables may scroll in
their own container. External assets, animation and a JavaScript build step are unnecessary.
